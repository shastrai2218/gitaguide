# Welcome to the GitaGuide closed beta

Thank you for being one of a small group of invited testers. This page tells
you what GitaGuide is, what to try, and how to tell us what you find.

## What GitaGuide is

GitaGuide is a reflection companion. You describe a situation that is weighing
on you — a decision, a conflict, a worry — in English or Hinglish. GitaGuide
reads the emotion in your message, retrieves relevant verses from the
Bhagavad Gita and the Arthashastra, and answers with a grounded reflection,
showing you exactly which verses it drew from.

It is **not** a doctor, therapist, counsellor, or astrologer, and it can be
wrong. It offers perspective, not advice you should follow blindly, and never
medical or mental-health treatment.

## What to try

- Bring real (or realistic) situations: a career dilemma, a family
  disagreement, a decision you keep postponing, a worry about money or duty.
- Write the way you naturally write — English, Hinglish, long or short.
- Have at least one longer conversation (8–10 messages) so the session
  memory gets exercised.
- Read the cited verses. Tell us when a citation felt apt — and especially
  when it felt irrelevant or forced.

## What to expect

- Replies take **5–25 seconds**. The screen tells you what is happening while
  you wait. If it says the model is under load, it genuinely is — please wait
  rather than resending.
- The first message after a quiet period can take up to a minute — that's the
  free hosting waking up, not a crash.
- If you send many messages very quickly you may be asked to wait a minute.
  That's a limit protecting the beta, not an error.
- If your message expresses serious distress, GitaGuide will step aside and
  point you to real human support instead of scripture. That is deliberate.

## If you are struggling

This beta is not a crisis service. If you are in distress, please reach:

- **Tele-MANAS: 14416** — 24×7, free, Government of India mental-health
  helpline, available in multiple Indian languages
- **KIRAN: 1800-599-0019** — 24×7 national helpline
- **Emergency: 112**

## How to give feedback

- Use the 👍 / 👎 under any reply — one tap, with an optional comment.
- For anything bigger (a confusing screen, a reply that felt wrong or unsafe,
  a bug), message Kunj directly with a screenshot and roughly what you typed.
- The single most valuable thing you can report: **a moment where GitaGuide's
  answer felt unsafe, hurtful, or badly matched to what you wrote.**

## The fine print

Your conversations are stored and may be read by the developer to improve the
beta. Please don't type passwords, financial details, or anything you wouldn't
want a developer to see. Full details are in the Privacy Notice and
Disclaimer linked from the app's first screen.

Thank you — your honest use of this is what makes it better.
